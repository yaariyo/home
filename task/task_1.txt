Task 1: Define Project Scope & Vision

Context:
Yaariyo aims to be a friendship-first, India-centric social media platform, leveraging the Posty Next.js template. The platform must be scalable, mobile-friendly, open-source, and prioritize trust, cultural relevance, and local engagement.

Acceptance Criteria:
- A clear, documented project vision statement is created and approved by stakeholders.
- The vision addresses multi-lingual usage, vibrant communities, and compliance with Indian IT rules 2021.
- The scope includes scalability, mobile-friendliness, and open-source principles.

Subtasks:
1. Draft a project vision statement reflecting the above goals.
2. Review and refine the vision with input from stakeholders.
3. Document the final scope and vision in the project repository.
4. Ensure alignment with Indian IT Rules 2021 and local engagement priorities.

Task 19: Implement Compliance - Proactive User Education

Context:
Indian IT Rules 2021 require platforms to proactively educate users about their rights and responsibilities. This includes onboarding tours, community policies, and accessible FAQs.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Onboarding tour educates users about platform rules, rights, and responsibilities.
- Community policies and FAQs are accessible in all supported languages.
- Users are regularly reminded of key policies and updates.

Subtasks:
1. Design and implement an onboarding tour covering user rights and responsibilities.
2. Draft and translate community policies and FAQs into all supported languages.
3. Add accessible links to policies and FAQs throughout the platform.
4. Set up periodic reminders or notifications for key policy updates.

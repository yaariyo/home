Task 30: Mobile and Low-Bandwidth Optimizations

Context:
To ensure accessibility and usability for all users, especially those on low-tier smartphones and slow networks, Yaariyo must be optimized for mobile devices and low-bandwidth scenarios.

Acceptance Criteria:
- The platform loads quickly and functions smoothly on low-end smartphones.
- Key features are accessible and usable on slow or unstable network connections.
- Mobile UI/UX meets modern standards for responsiveness and usability.

Subtasks:
1. Profile and optimize app performance for mobile devices.
2. Implement lazy loading, image optimization, and data caching strategies.
3. Test the platform on a range of devices and network conditions.
4. Refine mobile UI/UX for accessibility and ease of use.

Task 9: Implement Moderation & Trust Features

Context:
Moderation and trust are vital for user safety and platform compliance. Yaariyo must combine AI-powered and human-in-the-loop content filtering, and provide a simple, always-accessible report/flag button for users.

Acceptance Criteria:
- AI-powered content filtering is implemented for text, images, and multi-lingual content.
- Human moderation workflows are established for flagged content.
- Every post and comment has a prominent report/flag button.

Subtasks:
1. Integrate AI-based content filtering for posts and comments.
2. Develop human moderation panel and workflows.
3. Add report/flag buttons to all user-generated content.
4. Test moderation and trust features for effectiveness and compliance.

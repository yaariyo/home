Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Yaariyo (Posty/Next.js Foundation)
1. Project Scope & Vision
Yaariyo is a friendship-first, India-centric social media platform, leveraging the Posty Next.js template as the baseline. It is designed for multi-lingual usage, vibrant communities, and integrated compliance with Indian IT rules 2021. The platform is scalable, mobile-friendly, open-source driven, and prioritizes trust, cultural relevance, and local engagement.
2. Core Objectives
•	Launch a secure, user-friendly, India-oriented social platform.
•	Support Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, and English at minimum.
•	Empower user communities, creators, and local commerce.
•	Operate with full compliance to Indian IT Rules 2021 and related legal norms.
3. High-Level Features
1.	Account Management
o	Registration (email/mobile/social)
o	Multi-language onboarding
o	Profile edit, privacy controls, 2FA
2.	Posts & Engagement
o	Create (text, images, video), comment, like, share, report
o	Feeds (global, regional, interest, language-specific)
o	Support for hashtags, mentions, and media uploads
3.	Groups & Communities
o	Create/join groups based on interests, locations, or culture
o	Moderation controls for admins
4.	Notifications
o	Real-time, multi-device sync
o	Customizable notification settings
5.	Creator Ecosystem
o	Creator badges, analytics, and monetization (tips/gifts)
o	Challenges, polls, and festival themed events
6.	Social Commerce
o	Product showcase within communities
o	UPI links to local sellers
7.	Moderation & Trust
o	AI-powered and human-in-the-loop content filtering
o	Simple, always-accessible report/flag button
8.	User Privacy & Data Control
o	Granular sharing settings for posts and profiles
o	Download/delete account/data
9.	Admin Analytics
o	Dashboard for growth, engagement, flagged content
o	Regional/language insights
4. 100% Compliance with Indian IT Rules 2021
Compliance Requirement	Solution
Grievance Officer (India resident)	Appoint staff; publish email/contact
Timely content takedown (<36h) upon legal notice	Automated takedown queue; priority flag in admin UI
User reporting & flagging in-app	Prominent “report” button on every post/comment
Publish Terms, Privacy, and Guidelines	Multi-language pages in footer & onboarding
Resolve user complaints <15 days	Grievance admin ticket dashboard
Periodic transparency reports	Auto-generated, admin-reviewed public update system
Automated moderation for unlawful content (multi-lingual)	AI screening (keywords, images, language aware) + human review
Content and log record retention	Store logs securely for minimum period as mandated
Proactive user education on rights & responsibilities	Onboarding tour, community policies, FAQ
Data residency (if sensitive data required by law to be local)	Ensure DB hosted in India (or hybrid compliant)

6. User & Legal Experience Requirements
•	Every step of account creation/posting/reporting accessible in any supported language.
•	Reports/takedowns visible to admin within seconds for urgent action.
•	Moderation staff training in legal removal timelines and escalation procedures.
•	Clear interface for users to raise grievances, with transparent tracking.
•	Public policy and compliance documentation, regularly updated.
7. Documentation & Continuous Improvement
•	Update PRD as legal or market requirements change.
•	Document new community guidelines or compliance changes within release notes.
•	Regular review of moderation efficacy and user safety with executive stakeholders.
8. Success Metrics
•	100% documented and auditable compliance with Indian IT Rules 2021
•	<12 hours median government/legal takedown response
•	95%+ feature usage (posting/reporting) across all major Indian languages
•	Uptime 99.9%+; performance acceptable on low-tier Indian smartphones
•	User complaint resolution median time <7 days

Task 18: Implement Compliance - Content and Log Record Retention

Context:
Indian IT Rules 2021 require that content and log records are securely stored for a minimum period as mandated by law. This ensures traceability and compliance in case of investigations or audits.

Acceptance Criteria:
- All user-generated content and logs are securely stored for the legally required retention period.
- Data storage is secure and access-controlled.
- Retention and deletion policies are documented and enforced.

Subtasks:
1. Implement secure storage for content and log records.
2. Define and enforce data retention and deletion policies.
3. Document retention policies for compliance and audits.
4. Test storage and retention systems for security and reliability.

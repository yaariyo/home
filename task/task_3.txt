Task 3: Implement Account Management Features

Context:
Account management is foundational for Yaariyo, enabling users to register, onboard in their preferred language, and manage their profiles securely. This includes registration via email, mobile, or social login, multi-language onboarding, profile editing, privacy controls, and two-factor authentication (2FA).

Acceptance Criteria:
- Users can register using email, mobile, or social accounts.
- Onboarding is available in all supported languages.
- Users can edit their profiles and set privacy preferences.
- 2FA is available and functional.

Subtasks:
1. Develop registration flows for email, mobile, and social logins.
2. Implement multi-language onboarding screens and logic.
3. Build profile edit and privacy control interfaces.
4. Integrate and test two-factor authentication.
5. Conduct user testing for all account management features.

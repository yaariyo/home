Task 13: Implement Compliance - User Reporting & Flagging

Context:
To comply with Indian IT Rules 2021 and ensure user safety, Yaariyo must provide a prominent, always-accessible report/flag button on every post and comment. This enables users to easily report content for review.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Every post and comment has a visible report/flag button.
- Reported content is immediately visible to admins for urgent action.
- The reporting workflow is simple and accessible in all supported languages.

Subtasks:
1. Add report/flag buttons to all posts and comments in the UI.
2. Implement backend logic to log and queue reported content for admin review.
3. Ensure reporting workflow is available in all supported languages.
4. Test the reporting and flagging process for usability and compliance.

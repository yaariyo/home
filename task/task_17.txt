Task 17: Implement Compliance - Automated Moderation for Unlawful Content

Context:
To comply with Indian IT Rules 2021, Yaariyo must proactively moderate unlawful content using AI screening (keywords, images, language-aware) and human review, supporting multiple Indian languages.

Acceptance Criteria:
- AI-based moderation is implemented for text, images, and multi-lingual content.
- Human review workflows are in place for flagged content.
- Moderation system is effective in detecting and handling unlawful content.

Subtasks:
1. Integrate AI screening for keywords, images, and language detection.
2. Develop human-in-the-loop review workflows for flagged content.
3. Support moderation in all supported Indian languages.
4. Test moderation system for accuracy, speed, and compliance.
